## Implementation

- `src/templates.mjs`, `src/pages.mjs`, `src/data.mjs`: grouped government navigation, icon specification rows and accessible legend, official leadership, shared editorial notice area and verified benefit records. Regenerated all 18 HTML inputs.
- `src/hero.mjs`, `src/carousel.js`, `src/carousel.css`: viewport-aware stage, deliberate mobile framing, 800 ms crossfades, visible 44 px controls and explicit pause/resume. Focus and manual input persistently pause rotation. Hover, offscreen position and hidden documents temporarily hold rotation. Inactive slides are inert and hidden from assistive technology. Deferred sources avoid fetching every hero image initially.
- `src/trust.js`, `src/motion.js`: three stable slots, one replacement about every six seconds, 360 ms fade/movement, independent pause and reduced-motion support. Shared transition timestamps prevent the two systems from moving together.
- `src/main.js`, `src/style.css`: single-row scrolling filters, overflow cues, chosen-button visibility, menu and dialog focus handling, measured header height, 200% text layout and shared visual refinement.
- `scripts/check-browser.mjs`: optional browser regression checks using an existing Playwright installation, without adding project dependencies or a test framework.

## Factual and tooling limits

KAL's direct rendered news page was empty during this review, whereas the indexed official listing and official PDF retained the dated recruitment notice. It is therefore presented as closed historical recruitment. The official home also carries incompatible age/customer totals; those totals are not used.

ISO 9001:2015 is stated on the official manufacturing page, and the masthead links to KAL's certification page. Retrieval of the linked certificate PDF failed, so independent verification of its validity period remains outstanding. Some official document endpoints returned fetch errors; their published official destinations are preserved.

The QA uses headless Chromium and Lighthouse on a local build. It does not establish physical-device coverage, a formal WCAG certification or production performance. Hidden-tab timing is tested with a simulated visibility event. Touch swipes are checked with pointer events; physical touch hardware remains untested. No media player is added; the existing official photo gallery provides authentic media without a fake play action.

See `QA.md`, `browser-results.json`, `accessibility-results.json` and `screenshots/refinement/` for final verification evidence.
